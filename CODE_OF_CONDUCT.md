# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual orientation.

Expected behavior includes:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respecting different viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users.
- Showing empathy toward other community members.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, sexualized language or imagery, publishing private information without permission, and other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct. They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that violate it, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation for serious or repeated violations.

Report unacceptable behavior privately through the repository's contact or security reporting channels. Do not include sensitive personal information in a public issue.

Reports will be reviewed and handled as confidentially as practical. Maintainers will communicate the outcome when appropriate.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
